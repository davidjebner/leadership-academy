**Notes**
- Always have an agenda.
- Someone owns each thing, and there's always a second person who can step into that role.
- People should know which decisions they can make and which need your approval.
- Many people don't believe they're allowed to make decisions.
- With a good process, you might not need to hire someone for it. Fix the process before adding a person. It's better to hire someone to make the process better.
- When people keep asking you to repeat things, don't show frustration to the team. Instead: "I'm losing time repeating these to you. How can I help you remember so neither of us loses time? I'm sure asking me each time costs you time too."
