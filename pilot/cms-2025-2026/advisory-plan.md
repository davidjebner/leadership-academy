# Cybersecurity Marketing Society: Business Advisory Plan

> This is a copy of `originals/CMS_Business_Advisory_Plan.docx`. It's the plan the pilot cohort was built on.

**Advisor:** David J Ebner
**Executive leadership:** Gianna Whitver, David Algava, Maria Velasquez
**Participants:** *(not yet defined in the plan)*

**Goal:** Turn CMS into an organization that any one person could walk away from and everything runs as it should.

## Curriculum

| Topic | Book | Hours | Workshop focus |
|-------|------|-------|----------------|
| Start with Why | *Start with Why: How Great Leaders Inspire Everyone to Take Action* | 7 | Mission, Vision, Values workshop |
| Leadership | *Tribal Leadership* | 8 | Leading vs. managing; skills of an effective leader; tactics of an effective leader |
| Client Success | *Delivering Happiness* | 7 | Primary, secondary, and tertiary clients. What does each like and dislike? How do we become a catalyst for more of what they like? |
| Time Management | *The 7 Habits of Highly Effective People* | 8 | Four quadrants; delegation matrix and workflow; time self-care (calendar management, away notices, etc.) |
| Business Processes | *The Toyota Way* | 8 | Develop operational units; build SOPs, including communication flow and rules; turn SOPs into project-management templates; train |
| Resource Deployment | *Creative Confidence* | 7 | Staff structure that fits our processes and goals; which tools help and which don't; expense review |
| Revenue and Growth | *Unreasonable Hospitality* | 6 | Product/value alignment; solution expansion |
